# Cylindrical Lens Base (clbase1)

![Cylindrical Lens Base (clbase1)](preview.png)

PCB-based optical bracket holding the first cylinder lens with a focal length of 75 mm  securely in position along the scanhead optical beam path.

## Key Details

* **Function:** Cylindrical lens mounting bracket
* **Material:** Rigid FR4 for thermal and mechanical stability
* **Interface:** Interlocking slots for alignment inside the scanhead chassis

*Part of the [Hexastorm (Firestarter)](../../README.md) open-hardware laser direct imaging project.*
