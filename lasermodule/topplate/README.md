# Scanhead Top Plate

![Scanhead Top Plate](preview.png)

Top enclosure cover plate in FR4. Seals the optical cavity to prevent dust ingress and optical contamination on the spinning polygon prism and lenses.

## Key Details
* **Role:** Top protective dust cover of scanhead
* **Material:** 1.6mm FR4 PCB material
* **Assembly:** Interlocking tabs for solder/tab joint assembly
