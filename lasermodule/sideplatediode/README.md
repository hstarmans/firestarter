# Scanhead Diode-Side Plate

![Scanhead Diode-Side Plate](preview.png)

Structural side wall of the scanhead housing specifically accommodating the laser diode heatsink and mounting hardware.

## Key Details
* **Role:** Diode-side enclosure wall
* **Features:** Laser diode barrel heatsink cutout and mounting clearance
