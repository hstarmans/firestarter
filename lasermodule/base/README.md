# Scanhead Base Plate

![Scanhead Base Plate](preview.png)

Structural bottom floor of the laser scanhead module. Hosts mounting holes for the prism motor assembly, internal optics brackets, and kinematic docking interfaces.
A female pinheader is shown but this one is normally not placed.

## Key Details

* **Role:** Bottom structural plate of scanhead housing
* **Material:** 1.6mm FR4 PCB material
* **Features:** Prism spindle mounting, beam pass-through cutouts, interlocking tabs

---

*Part of the [Hexastorm (Firestarter)](../../README.md) open-hardware laser direct imaging project.*
